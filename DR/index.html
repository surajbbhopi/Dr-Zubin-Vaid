About Doctor

Dr Zubin M. Vaid

M.D(Medicine)

Trusted Physician. Compassionate Caregiver. Dedicated Healer.

With over 25 years of experience in Internal Medicine and Critical Care, Dr. Zubin Mehernosh Vaid has been helping patients navigate complex health conditions with confidence, clarity, and compassion.

Known for his calm demeanor and meticulous clinical approach, Dr. Vaid believes that every patient deserves personalized attention and evidence-based medical care. Over the years, he has successfully treated thousands of patients with conditions ranging from diabetes and hypertension to infectious diseases, respiratory illnesses, thyroid disorders, and critical care emergencies.

Patients often appreciate not only his medical expertise but also the time he takes to listen, explain, and guide them through their healthcare journey.

Driven by a commitment to lifelong learning and ethical medical practice, Dr. Vaid combines modern medicine with a holistic understanding of patient well-being, helping individuals achieve better health and a better quality of life.

Dr. Zubin M. Vaid believes in building long-term relationships with patients and their families based on trust, transparency, and compassionate healing. His philosophy of care extends beyond treating illnesses—he strives to ensure that every patient feels cared for, supported, and reassured before, during, and after their consultation. It was with this vision that Niraant Clinic was established. The word Niraant signifies a state of peace, tranquillity, and freedom from worry. Besides providing treatment based on scientific evidence-based medicine, our aim is that every individual experiences this feeling from the moment they walk through our doors—a sense that their concerns are heard, their health is in caring hands, and they can leave with greater comfort, confidence, and reassurance

Treating People, Not Just Diseases

Good healthcare begins with understanding.

Dr. Vaid believes that every patient is unique, and so is their health journey. Rather than focusing only on symptoms, he strives to understand the complete picture - medical history, lifestyle, concerns, and long-term health goals.

His approach is built on three simple principles:

Listen Carefully

Every patient deserves to be heard. Understanding concerns is the first step toward accurate diagnosis and effective treatment.

Treat Thoughtfully

Evidence-based medicine, combined with clinical experience and personalized care, forms the foundation of every treatment plan.

Support Continuously

Healthcare doesn't end after a consultation. Dr. Vaid believes in building lasting relationships with patients and guiding them toward long-term wellness.

Laboratory & Diagnostic Support
  • Discounted and Comprehensive Pathology Testing

    • Comprehensive blood and diagnostic testing services through reputed and accredited pathology laboratories ensuring accurate, reliable, and timely reports.   • ABG

    • Rapid ABG testing facility for assessment of oxygen levels, respiratory function, and acid-base balance in emergency, critical care, and respiratory conditions.

  • Digital X-Ray

    • Digital X-Ray services providing clear imaging, faster processing, and accurate reporting for chest, bone, spine, and general radiological evaluation.

  • ECG & Cardiac Triage

    • ECG and cardiac triage services for prompt assessment of chest pain, palpitations, hypertension, breathlessness, and other cardiac symptoms with immediate medical evaluation

  • 2D-ECHO

    • Advanced 2D Echocardiography for detailed evaluation of heart structure and function including valvular heart disease, cardiomyopathy, heart failure, and hypertension-related cardiac conditions.

  • Sonography & Doppler Studies

    • Comprehensive sonography and Doppler studies for abdominal, pelvic, vascular, thyroid, obstetric, and organ-specific evaluation performed by experienced radiology specialists.

  • House Visits

    • Medical consultations in the comfort of your home strictly for follow up of elderly, bedridden, post-operative, patients when it is not possible to visit the clinic. House visits are available by prior appointment and subject to the doctor’s availability.

    • Rapid ABG testing facility for assessment of oxygen levels, respiratory function, and acid-base balance in emergency, critical care, and respiratory conditions.

  • Online Consultations (only for select follow up and outstation patients)

    • Consult Dr Zubin Vaid from the convenience of your home through online consultation. This service is restricted to a select patient follow-ups when it is not possible to visit the clinic.

  • Vaccination Clinic

    • Adult Vaccination based on CDC guidelines, tailored as per age, sex, co-morbidities, occupational risks and immune status. Vaccines are appropriately procured and administered under medical supervision.   • Cancer Screening

    • Early detection can significantly improve outcomes in many cancers. We offer risk assessment, clinical evaluation, and guidance for appropriate screening tests for common cancers, with timely specialist referral whenever required.

  • Physiotherapy Services

    • Individualised basic physiotherapy and rehabilitation programmes for musculoskeletal pain, joint and spine conditions, mobility problems, post-operative recovery, and age-related functional limitations. Treatment plans are designed according to each patient’s specific needs and recovery goals.

    • Dietary nutritional counselling for diabetes, obesity, hypertension, dyslipidaemia and metabolic disorders, is done at Niraant Clinic

  • Acute minor wound and Pain management (During clinic hours)

    • Prompt assessment and initial management of acute pain, minor injuries, cuts, wounds and dressings are available during clinic hours. Patients requiring further investigations or advanced care are appropriately guided and referred.

  • Arranging appointments for all faculties and ambulance services.

    • We assist patients in arranging appointments (as per individual patient needs) with trusted specialists across various medical and surgical faculties, helping ensure timely access to appropriate care. Assistance in coordinating ambulance services is also available whenever required.
